Lisa Manoban

Pranpriya Manoban also Lalisa Manoban. She is born on March 27th, 1997. She is the only child of her Thai mother and Swiss Step father. From a young age, Lisa gained an interest in the South Korean pop industry, admiring artists such as Big Bang and 2NE1, and wishing to someday follow down a similar path. In 2010, Lisa auditioned to join YG Entertainment in Thailand. Among the 4,000 applicants, she was the only individual to pass. She officially joined the label as their first foreign trainee on April 11, 2011.

She is the main dancer and rapper of Blackpink. She danced to lots of songs including "Attention" by Charlie Puth. Her balance, sense of rhythm ,grace and well controlled dance movements made her a great dancer.

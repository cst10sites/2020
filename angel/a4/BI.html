History of Blackpink

Blackpink,commonly stylized as BLACKPINK or BLΛƆKPIИK,is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment, consisting of members Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. The group debuted on August 8, 2016, with their album.

In 2016-2017: Blackpink was the first girl group to debut of YG after 2NE1 for six years.In 2018-2019: They had international recognition with a World Tour and new album. In January 2018, it was announced that the debut Japanese EP of the group would be re-released under the name Re:Blackpink. The digital version included the same songs as the original release, while the physical version included a DVD containing all music videos and six songs in the Korean language.

Their first album released is square one which includes "whistle" and "boombayah" on August eighth 2016.Their second album released is square two which includes "Playing with Fire" "Stay" and "Whistle" (Acoustic Version) on November 1, 2016. Their third album is Blackpink, a Japanese Debut Mini Album which includes "Boombayah" "Whistle" "Playin With Fire" "Stay" "As If It’s Your Last" and "Whistle" (Acoustic Version) released on August 30, 2017. Their fourth album is Re:Blackpink which is a Repackage Album Of Japanese Debut Mini Album and it includes "Boombayah" (Japanese version)"Whistle" (Japanese version)"Playing With Fire" (Japanese version)"Stay"(Japanese version)"As If It’s Your Last"(Japanese version)"Whistle"(Acoustic version/Japanese version)"Boombayah" "Whistle" "Playing With Fire" "Stay" "As If It’s Your Last" "Whistle" (Acoustic Version)released on March 28th, 2018. Their fifth album is Square up which is a mini album which includes "Ddu-Du, Ddu-Du" "Forever Young" "Really" "See U Later" "As If It’s Your Last" (Physical edition hidden track) on June 15,2018. Their sixth album is Blackpink in Your Area which is a Japanese Mini Album that includes "Boombayah" "Whistle" "Playing With Fire" "Stay" "As If It’s Your Last" "Ddu-Du, Ddu-Du" "Forever Young" "Really" "See U Later" and it is released on November 23, 2018. Their seventh album is Kill This Love which is a mini album that includes "Kill This Love" "Don't Know What To Do" "Kick It" "Hope Not" "Ddu-Du, Ddu-Du"(Remix).

Jennie Kim

Jennie Kim is a South Korean singer and rapper. She debuted as a member of Blackpink in August 2016 under YG Entertainment. She was born on January 16, 1996 and when she was eight her family took her on a tour to New Zealand and Australia. She went to international school in New Zealand and stayed in a home stay family. Jennie heard about K-pop while studying in New Zealand, finding a particular interest in YG Entertainment's music. Her mother was planning to transfer her to the United States in Florida at the age of 14 so she could become a lawyer or teacher, but Jennie called her mother crying to go against the idea. J ennie thought she might not be able to find work she liked while studying alone in Florida; her family supported her decision and she moved back to South Korea. Jennie participated in an audition held by YG Entertainment that same year and performed Rihanna's "Take a Bow". She later succeeded in joining the label as a trainee. Jennie said that she was so afraid of strangers and terrible at taking the initiative to be funny, that she could barely say "Hello" during her audition. She initially auditioned as a vocalist, but the company thought she should be a rapper instead since she was the only trainee capable of speaking English, and most of the songs she covered included raps.

Jennie's solo debut was announced by YG Entertainment on October 27, 2018 through a promotional poster on various social media accounts. A 23 second teaser video swas uploaded one day later onto Blackpink's official YouTube channel. The clip shows Jennie lying on a bed and repeatedly mentioning her name. The second teaser was uploaded one week later, showing her performing in various clothes in a photo studio. The third and last teaser was uploaded four days prior to the singles release, showing her with different outfits in various locations. The single was released digitally on November 12. It became viral on Youtube, even its dance video has received over 150 million views and 2.3 million likes on Youtube.

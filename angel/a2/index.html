Recipe of Candied Hawthorn

I will be making my favorite food, candied Hawthorn. It is my favorite food because when I was in China, everyday after school there would be an old lady selling then in front of my school and whenever I have time, I wouold buy one and enjoy it by myself.

Equippments needed

  • Hawthorn
  • Water
  • Sugar
  1. First,rinse ½ cup of Hawthorn
  2. Then,boil the 150ml of water and 150grams of sugar in the pot to 140oC-160oC
  3. Then,put the Hawthorn on a stick and stick them in the pot of the sugar liquid
  4. Finally,take them out of the pot and wait for them to cool down.

Poor Man's Beef Stroganoff

Introduction

I am doing Poor Man's Beef Stroganoff, and the ingedients are cheap which is where the "Poor Man's" part of the title comes from. Beef stroganoff is a dish with meat and sauce on noodles. This is my favorite food to make because it tastes delicious and it is the main food I remember to make. I chose this dish because I was taught how to make it from my father.

Ingrediants

  • 2 Cans of cream of chicken soup
  • 2 ½ cans of water
  • 1 Medium onion
  • 700 grams of lean ground beef
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 3 ½ doleps of high fat sour cream
  • 500 grams of pasta
  • Sweet or Hot Paprika
  • Pinch of Rosemary
  • Tarragon
  • Sea Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Put both cans of cream of chicken soup in a medium pot, turn on stove to a bit above low.
  2. Put 2 half cans of water in each can scraping off every bit of cream of chicken soup, put into the medium pot.
  3. Chop mushrooms finely add to the medium pot.
  4. Cut onion and clove of garlic finally, put into frying pan with stove on medium.
  5. In a frying pan brown the onions whilst also cooking the garlic, when brown add to the medium pot.
  6. Fry beef in the frying pan adding fresh ground sea salt and pepper.
  7. When beef is mostly cooked (a bit might still be red) but not too dry add to pot, including the fat from the frying pan.
  8. Turn the medium pot up to medium, put on top but letting water vapour to escape, stir occasionally.
  9. Add rosemary and/or terragon.
  10. Cook pasta in a big pot.
  11. Drain pasta in colander.
  12. Once mushrooms are fully cooked, turn stove to low and add around 3 ½ doleps of sour cream. Do not let it boil but just before.
  13. Serve pasta adding sauce.
  14. Add sweet or hot paprika just before eating.

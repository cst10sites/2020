Habitats

Penguins naturally only live in the southern hemisphere as shown as below:


Penguins habitats have 2 different varieties for raising babies. King and Emperor Penguins lay eggs and put them on their feet to keep them warm.

Other penguins such as Adelie and Snares Penguins make nests to put their eggs in and stay ontop of the eggs to keep the egg warm.

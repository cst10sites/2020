The 20 Types of Penguins

There are most comonly 20 types of penguins. it is debated on the exact number of penguin species, some small differences related to regions can classify a penguin as a different type. For example there was originally just Rockhopper Penguins, but now there is Northern, Eastern, and Southern Rockhopper. This list is just including alive penguins that are not debated on if they are a type.

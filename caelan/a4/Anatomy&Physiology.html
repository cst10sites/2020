Anatomy & Physiology

Penguins are adapted to aquatic life. Their wings have evolved to become flippers, useless for flight in the air. In the water, penguins are very agile. Penguins are camoflaged which is the white and black feathers. Predators below swimming penguins see the white bellies which would be the same color of the sky if underwater. Above swimming penguins you would see the black feathers which blends in with the dark water.

Penguin skeletons are very similar with differenceas depending on the type of penguin. This is a skeleton of an Emperor Penguin. Other penguin skeletons would mainly be different by the size of the bones.

Did You Know

Penguins have knees. When penguins are standing up they visually appear to be squatting.

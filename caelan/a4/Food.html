Food

Penguins eat fish, crustaceans, and cephalopods. Fish they specifically eat are cod, sardines, anchovies, and more. Crustaceans they eat are krill and shrimp. Cephalopods that penguins eat are squid.

Majour parts of penguins' diets is fish. Healthy schools of fish are essential for a waddle of penguins to survive. Crustaceans and cephalopods make up smaller parts of some penguins' diets.

Did You Know

A group of penguins in the water is called a 'raft', a group of penguins on land is called a 'waddle'. Other collective nouns for penguins include rookery, colony, and huddle.

Introduction

My favorite food is Spaghetti with cheese. It is my favorite food because of its taste. I eat this food with some meat and a small salad with cucumbers and tomatoes.

Ingredients you need for Spaghetti with cheese:

  • 200g of Italian Spaghetti
  • 20g of salted butter
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 2L of water
  • 1 teaspoon of olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons of Parmesan Cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil 2½L of water at 100°C and fill up ½ of the large 5L pot. To make sure spaghetti don't stick together, put 1 teaspoon of olive oil in the pot.
  2. Add 1 teaspoon of salt in the pot. The salty water adds flavor to the spaghetti.
  3. Pour spaghetti into boiling water. Don’t break them; they will soften up later and fit into the pot. Then stir it well but carefully so spaghetti don't stick to each other or to the pot.
  4. After 8-10 minutes taste spaghetti before draining to make sure the texture is right. It should be soft but a little chewy.
  5. Drain cooked spaghetti well in a colander. Then serve them hot, add 20g of salted butter right away so it gets melted and 2 teaspoons of Parmesan Cheese on top.

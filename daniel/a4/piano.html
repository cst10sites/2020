How I started

I began playing the piano when I was 5 years old and I have been playing since then weekly. I am currently at grade 8 in RCM. Piano is one of the two activities that I still being taking since I was very young. It is a lot of fun and challenge to play piano and you have to start learning it before the age of 5-7 years old afterwards you will not have the right fingering to play the piano. When I was 5 years old I have been taking group piano lessons Kodali method at Vancouver academy of music for 2 years. Then I started private lessons at Jean Lyons School of Music and I have enjoyed playing piano at the school because of the amazing teachers feedback to the students which makes it quite easy to continue growing.

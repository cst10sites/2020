Piano in Entertainment

Piano has been used in Entertainment since its founding in the 1700s. The Piano could effectively create emotional works that lots of people could enjoy. It has been used in bands concerts for solos and playing with the band, in competitions for showing of piano skills of playing notes, beauty and general feel of the song or in the public to entertain people. In the Public it is also used to make a random person who starts playing the piano to get into the spotlight.

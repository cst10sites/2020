Fermi Paradox Theories

There are many theories created by scientist in an attempt to solve the Fermi Paradox, but they are mainly organized into 2 groups.
Group 1: There’s no sign of life out there because there are no higher civilizations that exist.
Group 2: Intelligent civilizations are out there, there’s a reason why we haven’t heard from them yet.

Theories - Group 1

Group 1 mainly discusses the Great Filter theory. The great filter is a disaster or evolutionary leap that is so great that all or nearly all civilization fail to pass it. It is something that limits the development of intelligent civilizations. This could appear in many forms, for example, it could be a nuclear war that wipes out all the planet’s inhabitants, a natural disaster, biochemical warfare, or out of control AI. This leads 3 possible realities, depending on where the great filter is.

Possibility 1:
the great filter is behind us, we are rare.
This possibility suggests that we have already passed the great filter, something that is extremely hard for other civilizations to overcome. This leads to the question, which event was it? Was it the ice age, or the leap from a simple prokaryote cell to a complex eukaryote cell, or was it the fact that our planet collided with a mars sized planet 4.5 billion years ago? Very few civilizations have passed the great filter, making us very special and unique. But there could also be other civilizations that might have passed the great filter as well.

Possibility 2:
We are the first.
The fact that the great filter might be behind us could also mean that we are the first to pass it. Maybe it’s because the universe was very uninhabitable in its early stages and that conditions have only improved recently for life to develop. It could be that in the early stages of the universe, gamma-ray bursts are very common, and any life that developed would be wiped out by cataclysmic events like gamma-ray bursts, supernovas or asteroids.

Possibility 3:
the great filter is ahead us, we’re screwed.
The 3rd possibility suggests that we haven’t yet passed the great filter and that we are pretty much doomed. Perhaps it’s climate change, pollution, a nuclear war, or a disaster we don’t yet know about. If the great filter theory is true then finding life on other planets would be really bad, because it would mean that we have yet to pass the great filter.

The Kardashev Scale

The Kardashev scale is a method of measuring a civilization's level of technological advancement based on the amount of energy they are able to use. The scale organizes civilization into 3 categories, Type I, Type II, and Type III.

A Type I Civilization has the ability to use all of the energy on their planet.
We’re not quite a Type I Civilization yet. We are currently estimated to be a Type 0.73 civilization.
A Type II Civilization can harness and utilize all of the energy of their home star.
It’s hard to imagine how they might be able to do so, but we’ve come with ideas like the Dyson sphere, which might allow us to harness the sun’s energy.
A Type III Civilization has unimaginable power; they have access to power comparable to that of the entire Milky Way galaxy.
They would have complete control over their galaxy. In other words, they would have colonized their galaxy.

So if there are Type III civilizations out there, then why haven’t we seen them yet?

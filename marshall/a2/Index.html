Chicken Noodle Soup


Introduction

Chicken noodle soup is a classic comfort food that is easy and cheap to make.
The ingredients are simple and can be found at your local supermarket.
Chicken noodle soup is also commonly used to relieve cold symptoms.
The vegetables in the soup strenghthens the immune system,
the chicken broth provides hydration and stimulates nasal clearance,
the chicken enhances the immune system and is low in fat and calories.
Research has shown that it may have a mild anti-inflammatory effect than can help ease symptoms.
I love chicken noodle soup because it warms your body and your soul while filling up your stomach.
It is also fairly easy to prepare when you are in need of a quick meal.
The following recipe will shown you how to make your own chicken noodle soup in 30 - 40 minutes (including prepping time)!

Ingredients

Adjust according serving size and preferences

  • 1 - 2 pounds of skinless, boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 - 4 medium sized carrots (chopped)
  • 2 - 4 celery stalks (chopped)
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 large onion (chopped)
  • 8 - 10 cups of chicken broth/stock
  • 5 - 8 ounces of noodles of choice (usually egg)
  • 1 tablespoon canola/olive oil or butter
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon minced fresh thyme or ¼ or ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice (optional)
  • ¼ - 1 teaspoon of pepper
  • ½ teaspoon of salt

    • Steps

    1. prepare the ingredients ahead of time. Chop the carrots, celery, onion, and parsley into sizable pieces. Mince the garlic and the thyme (or dried).
    2. Melt butter (or add oil) in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat (at around 162°C - 190°C) Add the (chopped) onions, carrots, and celery. Cook, stirring every few minutes until the vegetables begin to soften. This should take 5 to 6 minutes.
    3. Add garlic, bay leaves, and thyme to the mixture and stir. Cook, while stirring, for about 1 minute.
    4. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a low simmer. Taste the soup and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper. Depending on the stock used, you might need to add more or less teaspoons of salt.
    5. Submerge the chicken thighs into the soup so that the broth covers them. Partially cover the pot with a lid and cook, stirring a few times until the chicken thighs are cooked through This will take about 20 minutes.
    6. If the broth seems low, add a more stock or a bit of water. Turn the heat to medium-low.
    7. Transfer the cooked chicken to a plate. Add the noodles into the soup, stir and cook until done (6 to 10 minutes depending on the type of noodles used)
    8. While the noodles cook, shred the chicken into strips or dice into cubes. Add the chicken back into the pot and then taste the soup once more for seasoning. Adjust with more salt and pepper, as needed.
    9. Stir in the parsley (add lemon juice if you want) and serve.

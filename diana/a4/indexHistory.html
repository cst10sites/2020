All About Axolotls

Quick History of Axolotls

Axolotls (aka the mexican walking fish) are named after an Aztec God known as Xolotl.They are only native to Lake Xoxhimilco and Lake Chalco in Mexico. Sadly, Lake Chalco has been polluted and drained because of the near-by village and flood risks, so it no longer exists as a habitat for axolotls. Lake Xochimilco is the last place where axolotls can live in the wild. However, it is also threatened by pollution and captors.

Extinction Risks

Because of all of the threats mentioned above and more, axolotls are currently Critically Endangered on the Red List of Threatened Species. While axolotls may never go officially extinct because of their high population in labs, aquariums, and as pets, having axolotls go extinct in the wild is a tragic loss of diversity that must be avoided.

Helping Axolotls

There are many organization doing their best to help these adorable creatures survive in the wild. Shelters are being made, money is being donated, and people are volunteering to support axolotls around the world.

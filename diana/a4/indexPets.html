All About Axolotls

Can Axolotls be Good Pets?

Axolotls are fairly easy to take care of, so they are actually great pets for people with little or a lot of experience with exotic pets. Nevertheless, there are some special treatments axolotls need. Read more to find out some key notes about keeping an axolotl as a pet!

Finding an Axolotl

Because of their habitat requirements axolotls are not usually found in your local petstore. Don't be discouraged! You can still have an axolotl as a pet! You'll simply need to find a private breeder or a fellow axolotl enthusiast. Maybe you'll find one at a reptile show/expo or order them online. The most important thing is to make sure that the place you are getting your axolotl from is credible and raises them well.

Keeping and Caring for the Axolotl, Your new BFF

You will need an aquarium that can hold at least 10 gallons of water per axolotls. It can be full of only fresh water (no land) because axolotls cannot crawl on land. Axolotls can, however, jump out of the tank, so installing a mesh filter overtop wouldn't be a bad idea. The tank should contain natural rocks (but not too small, or they'll swallow), plants, and hiding spots. Don't forghet about a water filter! You'll need to change it frequently.

Most people don't know that axolotls are carnivores, so they need to eat meat. Though, you should avoid live feeder fish so the tank won't be contaminated. Axolotls main prey is baitworms and bloodworms, which can be purchased at fishing supply stores. If you want to give them a treat, frozen shrimp or raw hamburger balls are the way to go.

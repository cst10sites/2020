All About Axolotls

What's an axolotl?

An axolotl (ACK-suh-LAH-tuhl) ius very similar to a salamander, except that it retains its larval features througthout its whole 10-15 year life. That is called neoeny, which means that this exceptional organism keeps its dorsal fins (tadpole-like, not shark ones) and its feathery external gills, the most recognizable feature of an axolotl. While most people think axolotls are exclusively pink and white, that is not true. Most wild axolotls are muddy, dark colours - ranging from black to beige. Axolotls have many "superpowers", like regrowing limbs! More fun facts about axolotls are in this website!

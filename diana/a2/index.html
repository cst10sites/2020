My Favourite Recipe

Introduction:

This is a recipe that my beloved grandmother taught me a few weeks ago. Its name is "aladoushki." I've practiced it and made it multiple times since then to burn it into my memory. It is a delicious recipe that is very similar to pancakes, but they taste much better. They are more sweet than savoury and can be eaten plain or with jam, cream, sugar, or other normal pancake toppings. This is basically my test to see if I remember the recipe. Even if you probably won't make this, enjoy reading the recipe and hoping you have something to eat that is a s delicious as this!!!

-

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of homemade kefir
  • 2 eggs
  • 3-4 spoons of sugar
  • 1 pack of vanillin
  • A pinch of salt (Or about ¼ of a teaspoon)
  • 3-4½ cups of sifted flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Grapeseed (or some kind of cooking) oil (for the pan)
  • Optional: Toppings!!!

Instructions

  1. Pour the kefir into a big bowl, and mix in the eggs with a spoon until they are incorporated and a solid colour.
  2. Add the sugar, vanillin, and salt and stir for a bit.
  3. Sift in one cups of the flour and mix well until fully incorporated.
  4. Continue adding the flour in bigger portions until the batter has a consistency of "gustoya smyetana," or "thick sour cream". You may need more or less flour. This part varies a lot.
  5. Stir in the baking soda until incorporated, do not over mix.
  6. Let the batter stand for a few minutes while you prepare the pan.
  7. Turn on a stove element to high heat. Pour a thin layer of oil onto the pan and place spoonfulls of the batter onto the pan. Turn off the heat or set it to low.
  8. Keep an eye on the aladoushki and flip them when the batter forms bubbles or when you see fit. Basically, treat them like regular pancakes. When they are cooked, set them onto a platter lined with paper towel to absorb some of the oil (kind of optional) and make sure that there is some sort of covering or lid to retain fluffy-ness.
  9. Repeat steps 7-8 until all of the batter is used. If you are feeling risky, you can stuff berries or apple shreds inbetween the batter spoonings.
  10. Let them cool under a covering or lid to retain fluff, or hafashafahshahah them while they're hot (my favourite technique). Enjoy them with or without toppings!

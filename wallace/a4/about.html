History

Overwatch has been released in May 24, 2016. It already had 7 million players a week after it came out. Players that were professionals in other games came to Overwatch and some turned into pros, climbing in the highest ranks of Overwatch. A few months later, South Korea created the first actually "big" Overwatch tournament. It was called Overwatch Apex.

Overwatch started growing, and Overwatch Apex had more and more players interested in watching the tournament and more people started watching the matches. After almost two years, the Overwatch League was created and Overwatch Apex was over. At this time the Overwatch community grew up to 40 million active players. Overwatch was ranked the 7th best selling game in the whole of United States. Of course, this heavily increased the amount of viewers in the Overwatch League.

There were only 12 teams in the first season instead of the 20 teams we have currently in season 3. The League plans to have up to 28 teams in the distant future, and be able to play in bigger stadiums that can be fit with more people watching.

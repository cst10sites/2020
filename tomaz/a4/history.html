Dishes made from flatbreads were served for 100s of years, since the Neolithic Age, which consists the years of 4500 BC until 10000 AD. In the 6th century BC, Persian soldiers baked "flavored bread on top of their battle shields, using cheese and dates. Many kings and queens tasted the delicacy. Eventually in Naples, Italy, modern pizza was "invented" during the 18th and 19th century, served from pizza stands, later evolving into contemporary pizzerias.

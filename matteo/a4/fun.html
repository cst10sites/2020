Fun Facts

  • Venus' year is shorter than it's day, mostly due to it's proximity to the Sun
  • Comets travel outside of our solar system, but also come the closer to the sun than any planet
  • There are over 150 million asteroids with a greater than 100m diameter in our solar system
  • Light from the Sun takes 4 hours and 12 minutes to reach Neptune
  • There are 214 moons in our solar system
  • One day, Saturn's rings will dissipate
  • The Earth moves at around 107 000km/h around the Sun
  • Our solar system is about 4.6 billion years old
  • Neptune's poles are warmer than the rest of the planet during seasonal changes
  • Eventually, there will no longer be moons or all current moons will be replaced

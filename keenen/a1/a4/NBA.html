NBA

The National Basketball Association is a mens basketball league based in North America. The NBA has 30 teams which competes in the regular season that lasts 82 games. Once the regular seasons concludes, the NBA will move on to the playoffs where the top 8 teams of the East and West (16 teams total) compete to win the championship. The teams that make it to the playoffs will face another team in a first to four wins game before advancing to the next round of the playoffs then they will head to the confrece finals which will decide who plays in the NBA finals, the best team from each confrence compete for the championship.

Examples of Aviation in History

Aviation is the design, development, production, operation and use of aircraft <1>. However, this mainly covers one type of aircraft that is heavier than air. Confused? Keep in mind that the term aircraft covers all machines capable of flight <2>. In order to better comprehend the definition of aviation and aircraft, it is important to know that types of aircraft are seperated into two categories: heavier-than-air and lighter-than-air <2>. Lighter-than-air aircraft are designed to contain, within their structure, a gas lighter than air which will lift the aircraft up <3>. Heavier-than-air aircraft have some kind of engine that provide thrust which moves the aircraft through the air <4>. Heavier-than-air aircraft include airplanes, helicopters and gliders <4>. Lighter-than-air aircraft include airships and hot air balloons <4>. The history and types of these aircraft can all be explored here. Have fun learning new facts!

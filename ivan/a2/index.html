Introduction

One of the types of food that I sometimes eat is a dish called okroshka.

Okroshka is a kind of soup that my family likes to eat during summer.
I enjoy eating this dish due to the soup containing food such as vegetables and meat. As well as the broth being kvass, a traditional bread-like beverage of russian origin. This dish supplies me with protein, other vitamins, and a very weak dose of alcohol, which is enough to satisfy my hunger.

This is how okroshka looks like.

Ingredients:

  • Some Meat, about ½ a cup Usually beef or pork, with some chopped sausages.
  • Herbs, about ¼ a cup
  • Kvass, a cup It may contain alcohol.
  • And some sour cream, if you want ;)

Procedure:

  1. Add the meat into a mixing bowl
  2. Add the herbs into the mixing bowl with the meat
  3. Mix the two ingredients together
  4. Dump the contents of the mixing bowl carefully into a large bowl
  5. Pour in the kvass
  6. Serve cold and enjoy :)

If you really want to taste the alcohol, add vodka instead of kvass ;)

Introduction to Golf

Although golf may appear as a boring activity that is only played by wealthy elderly people, if you understand how to play it and how it works, you will realize how much fun and entertaining it is. Originally designed as an easygoing pasttime for the rich and powerful, golf has evolved into a nearly $70 billion industry, and has even made it into the Olympics!   • Rules of Golf

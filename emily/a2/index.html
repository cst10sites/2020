Instant Noodles with Eggs

Introduction

In Asia, instant noodles are a common source for a quick, delicious meal. There are many flavour variations, but I personally prefer the original flavour, as it embodies the true taste of instant noodles. Here in North America, instant noodles are becoming more and more common, although some of the Western versions of the beloved snack are not great to say the least. Today, as you probably guessed, we will be making instant noodles! I like to add an egg or two to my noodles to pack an extra punch, but your noodles will taste amazing eggless or not. The reason instant noodles are my favorite recipe is because its quick, easy, and delicious! Cup versions of instant noodles require no dishes at all, and most instant noodles are finished cooking in 5 minutes or less. In addition, the combination of the unique texture of the noodles and the excessive amounts of sodium in the soup make for an extremely tasty dish, although it may not be the healthiest. Ingredients

  • 100g Instant Noodles (I prefer Nissin brand)
  • Flavour Packet
  • 2 Eggs
  • ¾ Cups Water

Intructions

  1. Boil the water to 100°C.
  2. Take the noodles out of the package, and boil for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Pour the noodles and half of the water into a bowl.
  4. Open the flavour packet and mix it into the noodles.
  5. Crack two eggs into a bowl, and pour them into the remaining boiling water.
  6. Wait about 30 seconds, then pour the eggs into the noodles.
  7. Finally, break the egg yolk and mix it into the noodles.

Now you have delicious instant noodles! Happy eating!

Chocolate Mango with Nuts

Time: 20 minutes | Plus 10 Minutes prep.

Introduction

This recipe is theoretically possible, however I have never tried it. It still counts because if I did make it I would like it. It is a medium-large sized snack for any day. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • ⅛ Cup of Ground Nuts
  • Mango, halved and peeled
  • ¼ Cup of chocolate

Instructions

  1. Heat Choclate in a small bowl to 30° C
  2. Cool Mango to 5° C
  3. Pour melted chocolate, while still warm, on mango.
  4. Sprinkle nuts as chocolate cools.

Assignment 2: My Favourite Recipe

Introdution

Have you ever wanted to taste the divine greatness that is Nutella Stuffed Strawberries?

The sweet taste of nutella inbedded within strawberries will amaze you!

They are easy to make and looks delicious. They are also a great dessert for friends and family!They are both sweet and savoury!

Ingredients

  • 15 - 17 Strawberries
  • ½ cup of Nutella
  • ¼cup of coarsely chopped toasted almonds

Instructions

  1. Slice strawberries in half lengthwise.
  2. Using a 1/4 teaspoon, hollow out each strawberry half.
  3. Spoon or pipe in about a 1/2 teaspoon of Nutella into each half and sprinkle generously with chopped almonds.
  4. Serve immediately!

Conclusion

If you have done all of this correctly, you will get a great food! Enjoy it with friends & family!

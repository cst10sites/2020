Hot Chocolate Recipe

Introduction:

This is a simple recipe for some hot chocolate. It is my favourite recipe because it is super easy and fast to make, while also being tasty. Hot chocolate is also very customizable, you can add marshmallows, peppermint extract or whatever else you like!

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons sugar
  • ½ pinch of salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. In a small saucepan over medium-low heat, mix together the cocoa, sugar, salt and 2 tablespoons milk until dissolved.
  2. Mix in the remaining milk.
  3. Heat over medium heat until hot, mixing occasionally.
  4. Stir in the vanilla.
  5. Serve and enjoy!

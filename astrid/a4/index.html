    • This is Astrid's website for the teen novel trilogy Renegades by Marissa Meyer. It is a book series revolving
    around a world where people, known as Prodigies, have superpowers. The backstory is that Prodigies
    suddenly started being born, and were often rejected by their parents and society, so they teamed up
    and plunged the world into anarchy. However, some prodigies wanted to use their powers for good, so
    they teamed up in an organization called the Renegades to fight prodigies who use their powers for
    evil, and protect their cities. The thing is, our main character, Nova Artino, had her family killed
    in front of her by hired thugs at a young age, and despite her calling the Renegades, she had to use
    her power, which is putting people to sleep, on the hired thugs. She then gets found by
    her uncle, who happens to be the head of the Anarchists. So, she joins and lives with the Anarchists,
    becoming known as the villian Nightmare. However, she gets sent on a spy mission to become a Renegade
    in order to figure out their security system, under her second superpower, that she doesn't need sleep.
    Her Renegade alias is Insomnia, and she starts living a double life, and befriends a Renegade named Adrian,
    unaware he has a secret of his own. There are 3 books in the series: Renegades, Archenemies, and Supernova.

Taco

Time:15 mins

Introduction

My recipe is a taco. I love tacos because they taste good. the taco I will be making is a pork taco, not a baby taco. here is a picture of a baby taco.

Ingredients

  • Pork - 15 pieces(make sure that the pork is above 0°C
  • Taco Shell - 2(make sure is above 50°C
  • Cheese - 5 pieces
  • Lettuce - 10 pieces
  • Salsa - 15 grams

Utensils

  • Hand

Directions

  1. Place Pork in Taco shell
  2. Place Lettuce and Cheese on Pork
  3. Add some Salsa and you're done!

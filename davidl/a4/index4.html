Gamemodes

Bomb

This is the standard gamemode and only gamemode in ranked or unranked. Attackers have a defuser, they have to plant the defuser in one of the sites to win. Defenders can counter defuse, if all attackers get killed during post-plant defenders still have to defuse. Defuser takes 40 seconds to fully defuse the bomb.

Secure Area

Attackers have to secure the biohazard container. If you and an enemy player are in the site then the securing pauses. If your team leaves then the securing progress goes down every second until it is gone.

Hostage

Defenders try to prevent attackers to secure the hostage. When attackers grab the hostage 1 minute is added to the timer.

Ranked and Unranked

The best part of siege and it shows you skill, it is always bomb gamemode and best to 4 until it turns to overtime then it will be first to 5. The only difference between ranked and unranked is that ranked upgrades or downgrades you rank for every game.(depends if you win or lose)

Quick Match

Really fast paced shorter rounds, first to 3 wins. Any gamemode can be played and it is a really good gamemode to start off with.

Newcomer

I don't suggest this gamemode. There are hardly new players who play newcomer and people who play newcomer are already skilled people who bought another account of R6. I suggest quickmatch for faced paced learning and more renown gain.

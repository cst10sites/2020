Teams

Here are all of the biggest esport organizations with what games they are invested in:

Team Liquid - net worth ~$500 million. Games: StarCraft 2, League of Legends, Hearthstone, CS:GO, Dota 2, Super Smash Bros, PUBG, Quake, Rainbow 6 Seige, Clash Royale, Fortnite, Apex Legends and Auto Battler.

Team Solo Mid - net worth ~$600 million. Games: League of Legends, PUGB, Fortnite, Hearthstone, Super Smash Bros, Overwatch, Apex Legends, Magic the Gathering, World of Warcraft, Rainbow Six Seige and Teamfight Tactics.

Cloud9 - net worth ~$600 million. Games: Overwatch, CS:GO, Fortnite, Hearthstone, PUBG, Teamfight Tactics, League of Legends, Rainbow 6 Seige, World of Warcraft, Dota 2, Rocket League and Super Smash Bros.

FaZe Clan - net worth ~$350 million. Games: Call of Duty, Fortnite, Overwatch, CS:GO, Fifa, PUBG and Rainbow Six Seige.

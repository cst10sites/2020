How to order from Pizza Hut

Ingredients:

  • A phone
  • A phone number
  • Money

Steps

  1. Call Pizza Hut
  2. Ask for a large cheese pizza
  3. Tell the person where you live
  4. Answer the door to someone with a Pizza Hut uniform
  5. Pay them exactly $24.03 with a $5 tip
  6. Take the pizza and enjoy ½ the pizza at 35°C and save the rest for later

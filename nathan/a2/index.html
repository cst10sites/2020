Jambalaya

Jambalaya is a cajun dish that originated from the households of louisianna. The tomato based dish is mixed primarily with rice, and supplemented with meats and vegetable native to the louisianna coastline, such as shrimp, chicken, and onions. The dish is an explosion of exotic flavours that teleports you straight into the musty saloons of New Orleans. This dish is a delicacy in my family, and it brings bakc fond memories of family gatherings; its delicious, spicy, and fun to make. It is truly an Α (Alpha) recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 premade jambamlaya spice mix
  • 1-2 cans of diced tomatoes
  • However much rice depending on how many people you're serving
  • Shrimp, Chicken & a Spicy sausage
  • Bell-peppers and one Onion

Instructions

  1. Start rice in rice cooker & cook onions in fry pan between 350°F-400°F until brown
  2. Cook the sausage, peppers and/or chicken in the fry pan
  3. Add tomoatoes, mix, and the rice when cooked
  4. Finally! Add the shrimp
  5. Keep on stirring untill cooked, and enjoy!

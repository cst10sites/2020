My Favourite Character

Gandalf

As explained on the Fellowship page Gandalf is a wizard, but he is not your stereotypical wizard with a Hogwarts degree in wizardry. In fact, he is an angelic being confined to mortal flesh, and sent to Middle Earth to aid the free peoples. What makes Gandlaf such an inmpressionable charcater is the ingenious acting behind him, his traits, and his suprisingly remarkable strength for an old man. In all, rendering this badass grey beard immortal amongst the fanatics of the series.

Sir Ian Mckellan in an extraordinary reputable actor and is phenomenal for the role of the wise old wizard. Firstly, his natural kindly eyes protrays the gentle compassion and wisdom present in the characters persona. In addition, his low melodious voice is powerful song that can volontarily flucate from soothing to commanding. Lastly, his reputation for playing other action film characters such as Magneto in the x-men films only overqualifies him. In all, his superior acting ability and experience creates the perfect fit for the role of Gandalf, and the film series would be unimainable without him.

Gandalf is described in Tolkien's Unfinished Tales as the wisest being in the universe, for he learned about pity and sorrow from the valar Nienna. Consequently, from this education Gandalf has the ability to communicate to Frodo the importance of compassion as beings: "Do not be too eager to deal out death in judgment". Ultimately, it is this council that forces Frodo to later have sympathy for Gollum, and thus permits the latter to accomplish his role in the trilogy. Lastly, what is most impressive is Gandalf's abstinance from taking the ring; being a powerful being he understands the devastation that were to be wrought should he wield such a tool. It is Gandalf's leaderhsip, wisdom, and compassion that inspires the other members of the fellowship and countless beings in Middle Earth to contribute to the fight against Mordor.

Throughout the series Gandalf exhibits an exemplary courage and strength that him proclaims as an unrivaled inspiration in the hearts of the audience. The wizard, despite his age frequently prevails through multiple trials; for one, he defeats the fiery Balrog of Moria and then promptly returns as the purer Gandalf the white, and his heroic rescue of Helm's Deep from the monson of Saruuman's orcs. Complementarily, he leads the forces of Minas Tirith in their seemingly futile defense against Mordor; in all, securing him as an awe inspiring character that so many idolize.

To conclude, with the over equiped arsenal of Ian Mckellen and the compelling character of the Grey Wanderer, Gandalf by far the most galvinzing character. While in some scenes butchering waves of orcs he contrastly, gives council to several protagonist which greatly affects the outcome of the series. In all, the compassion and wisdom of such being is unbeknownst to human kind, and we can only aspire to be as virteous as Mithrandir.

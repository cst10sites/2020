History of Soccer

The Game of Football, or better known as soccer (USA, Canada) was thought to be originated more than 2000 years ago in ancient China, Greece and even Rome. Although it was traced back to China and the other countries, it was Britain who transitioned the game into Soccer which we now know today. As the game developed, more rules and rules were being created and put into place until in 1913 FIFA finally became a member of the International Football Association Board.

Rules Of Soccer

The objective of Soccer Is to score on the opposing net, by just using your feet in the 90-minute game. After the 45-minute point, there is something called half-time where a 15-minute break is taken. Each team consists of 11 players with up to 7 substitutes with one of the players being a goalkeeper. The more basic rules include:

  • 1) The field must be made up of artificial or natural grass.
  • 2) The ball must be round.
  • 3) Each game has 1 Referees and two linesmen.
  • 4) If no one has scored in the game, 30 minutes of extra time will be given. If no one has scored in those 30 minutes, then the game will result in a penalty shoot-out.
  • 5) The entire ball must cross the line to count as a goal.
  • 6) If a foul occurs, depending on how severe it is it may result in a red or yellow card.
  • 7) if a player receives a red card, they will not be allowed to play that game.
  • 8) If the ball goes out it will result in a throw in, but If it goes out in the goalie's side of the field then it will result in a corner kick.
  • 9) If the ball goes offside then it will result in a freekick for the other team.

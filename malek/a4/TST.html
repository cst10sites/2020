It is one of the best teams in England and has many famous players such as Sergio Aguero, David Silva, Yaya Toure and Micah Richards. Manchester City was founded in the year 1880 and joined FIFA in the year 1887. This team has been doing well for most of it's time and continues to make strides to make their team better.

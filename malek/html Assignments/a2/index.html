Introducing COOKIES!

Today, we will be making some delicious chocolate chip cookies with a unique texture where the outside is a bit crispy whereas the inside of the cookie is chewy and delicious. This Recipe is a home-made recipe used by hundreds and hundreds of people and they all seem to love it!

Picture of the Delicious Cookies!

Ingredients:

  • 2¼All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 Teaspoon Baking Soda
  • ½ Teaspoon Salt
  • 1 cup of butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon Vanilla
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup Coarsley chopped nuts

Instructions:

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°C
  3. Mix The flour, baking soda, salt
  4. In large bowl, beat softened butter and sugars with electric mixer on medium speed, or mix with spoon about 1 minute or until fluffy, scraping side of bowl occasionally.
  5. Beat in egg and vanilla until smooth. Stir in flour mixture just until blended (dough will be stiff). Stir in chocolate chips and nuts
  6. Onto ungreased cookie sheets, drop dough by rounded tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until light brown (centers will be soft). Cool 2 minutes; remove from cookie sheet to cooling rack. Cool completely, about 30 minutes. Store covered in airtight container.

Frisian Orange Cake (Fryske Oranjekoek)

The recipe is a recipe for a Frisian Orange Cake. The cake is a classic Frisian snack made often only during the summer. The cake is something I very much enjoy because it tastes frsh but also sweet and fruity at the same time.

Ingredients

  • 350 grams of flour
  • 200 grams of light brown sugar
  • 1 tea spoon of baking powder
  • 30 grams of room tempurature butter
  • 2 egg
  • ½ too ¾ centiliters water
  • 2 table spoons of aniseed
  • 1 table spoon of nutmeg
  • 1 orange flesh
  • 150 grams of almond meal
  • 150 grams of cristal sugar
  • ½ of a lemon's juice
  • 1 table spoon of currant juice
  • 100 grams of powder sugar
  • 200 milliliters of whipped cream
  • Pieces of mandarin orange and lemon for garnishing
  • Some salt

Procedure

  1. Cover a 22 centimeter squared baking pan with baking parchement
  2. Mix the flour, the brown sugar, the baking powder, the aniseed and the nutmeg in a bowl
  3. Add the orange flesh, the butter, 1 of the eggs and continue mixing. Slowly add water until the mixture becomes sticky and consistent texture
  4. Cover the bowl in plastic and let it rise for 10 minutes
  5. When you are waiting for the cake batter to rise, mix the lemon juice, cristal sugar, the last egg and the almond meal together in a seperate bowl
  6. Warm your oven to 165°C and seperate the cake batter into two even parts
  7. Put one of the two parts at the bottom of the baking pan and spread it evenly, put the egg-lemon-sugar mixture on top of the layer of cake batter amnd put the final half of cake batter on the top of the egg-lemon-sugar mixture
  8. Insert the pan with the cake into the oven for 30 minutes
  9. Mix the currant juice and powder sugar when you are waiting in order to make a glaze
  10. Remove the cake from the oven
  11. Let the cake cool for 10 minutes and cut the lemon and the orange into small chunks
  12. Put the glaze on the cake and spread it evenly across the top of the cake
  13. Put the whiped cream on the cake like as shown in the image above and add little chunks of lemon and orange on top of the columss of whiped cream

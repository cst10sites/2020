Pizzicato Techniques

Pizzicato is the action of plucking the string. There is two main types of pizzicato, the one performed by jazz musicians and the one performed by classical ones.

Here is a video that explains the way that jazz bass players use pizzicato. The Jazz bass pizzicato gives a full sound with a heavy attack. It creates a percussive thump sound.

And here is a video that explains the way that classical bass players use Pizzicato. The Classical pizzicato has a small attack and a long sustain, it is a sound more suited to blend with other instruments in the orchestral context.

Here is two videos explaining how to switch between pizzicato and arco, one for the french bow and one for the german bow. This techniques is primarily classical as the use of the bow in jazz is uncommon (it does however exist).

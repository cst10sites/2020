Basketball is arguably an all time favorite sport which has been around for some time. Basketball is a team sport which includes many simple-complex defenses and offenses. One can score by shooting into the opponent's hoop with a basketball. Normal shots would give you an additional 2 points. If you shoot from outside the three point line, you are rewarded with 3 points. There are also foul shots which equal to 1 point each. There are many different leagues for just about all ages.

Two of the many leagues there are is the National Basketball Association (NBA/WNBA) and Euroleague. These leagues have most of the best basketball players in the world. These players are chosen from many to participate in these basketball games. The world of basketball is truly amazing.

Omelette Recipe

omelette is a fairly simple breakfist menu you can make without hard works

Igredient

    requirements

  • 2~3 eggs
  • cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon butter
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • ⅔ tablespoon milk or water
  • Herbs (optional)

Instruction

  1. Crack the eggs into a small mixing bowl, then stir gently with a fork until well-beaten
  2. Add the milk or water, salt and pepper, and any herbs, and set aside
  3. Heat a pan over high heat until very hot (approximately 30 seconds)
  4. Add the butter, making sure it coats the bottom of the pan
  5. As soon as the butter stops bubbling and sizzling, slowly pour in the egg mixture
  6. Tilt the pan to spread the egg mixture evenly. Let eggs firm up a little
  7. about ten seconds shake the pan a bit and use a spatula to gently direct the mixture away from the sides and into the middle
  8. Allow the remaining liquid to then flow into the space left at the sides of the pan
  9. Continue to cook for another minute or so until the egg mixture holds together
  10. While the middle is still a little runny, add the filling
  11. Put in sautéed vegetables or fruit first, near the center, then sprinkle any cheese on top
  12. Tilt the pan to one side and use the spatula to fold approximately one third of the omelette over the middle
  13. Shake the pan gently to slide the omelette to the edge of the pan
  14. Holding the pan above the serving plate, tip it so the omelette rolls off, folding itself onto the plate
  15. DONE!

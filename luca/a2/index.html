Chocolate Lava Cake

Today I will be teaching you how to make a chocolate lava cake. It is a little bit more of a harder thing to learn but once you get it right it is the most satisfying thing in the world, and it is my favorite dessert to make.

Ingredients:

  • 1 Stick of butter
  • 2oz of bittersweet chocolate
  • 2oz of semi sweet chocolate
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla
  • ½ All purpose flour
  • 3¾ Powdered sugar
  • Vanilla for serving

Directions:

Prep time:10 minutes
Bake time 12-14 minutes
Total time:20 minutes
  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C)
  2. Microwave butter and bittersweet chocolate for a minute
  3. Pour into a bowl and wisk in the sugar until well blended
  4. Wisk eggs and egg yolks and then pour into bowl
  5. Pour flour in and put into custard cups
  6. Bake for 12-14 minutes
  7. Serve with vanilla ice cream

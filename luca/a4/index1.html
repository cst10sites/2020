The ballon d'or has been around for many many years. The initial idea came from a journalist called Gabriel Hanot and he initially thought of it to be awarded to the best performing players of the season. The fisrt ever winner of the ballon d'or was Stanley Matthews. The ballon d'or would be decided by the vote of soccer journalists from 1956-2006 then in 2007 coaches and national teams were aloud to vote. Originally it was an award for only player playing in Europe but in 2007 it became a global prize. In 2018 it was decided to also have a separate ballon d'or for the womens players and an award for young player of the year.
